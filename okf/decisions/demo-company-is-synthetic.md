# Why
"Empresa Demo S.A.C." has a fake RUC and synthetic contracts, labeled "datos de demostración". It carries the Rosa story, auto-approved claims and test disbursements. Real companies appear only in search and verification of their real public data, never attached to a fictional persona or shown as sanctioned on video. This answers the bases' "false data" rule and the reputational risk.

# Rejected alternatives
- **Run the whole story on a real company:** a fictional "Rosa" denied credit over a real firm's data is false data; claiming it would require that firm's consent.
- **No interactive demo:** jurors could not try the claim or the disbursement themselves, losing UX and Stellar points.
